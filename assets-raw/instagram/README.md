# Instagram assets (drop zone)

Source: https://www.instagram.com/dr.ghinayassine/

Drop images saved from her Instagram here, as-is (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC are all fine).
Filenames do not matter; they get renamed in Phase 1.

Optional but very useful:

- `captions.txt`: paste her Instagram bio and post captions here (one caption per block,
  separated by a blank line). Phase 1 extracts the services she actually mentions from it.
  Anything she never mentions stays a `[CONFIRM]` placeholder on the site.

Originals in this folder are never modified. Processed copies go to `public/images/`.
